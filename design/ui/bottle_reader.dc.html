THE KEEPER’S TRAIL — II
Whoever finds this — the light was out for eleven winters before we came, and the coast forgot it. We have not forgotten.

There is brass in the cellar and glass in the crates. Two pairs of hands will do what one pair cannot. Keep the lamp turning.
— the keeper before you
