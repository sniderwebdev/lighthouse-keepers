## Music

One dusk theme, on a loop (author decision, `NEXT.md` 2026-07-31.2). The theme in
play is whatever sits at `godot/assets/audio/music/dusk_theme.ogg`; the
alternatives are kept beside it so swapping is a copy, not a download.

| File | Title / author | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/audio/music/dusk_theme.ogg` | **Heavenly Loop** — isaiah658 | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/heavenly-loop |
| `assets/audio/music_candidates/heavenly_loop.ogg` | Heavenly Loop — isaiah658 | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/heavenly-loop |
| `assets/audio/music_candidates/winter_dust.ogg` | Winter Dust — hernandack | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/short-loops-background-music-pack |
| `assets/audio/music_candidates/a_brand_new_wisdom.ogg` | A Brand New Wisdom — hernandack | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/short-loops-background-music-pack |

CC0 asks for nothing, but isaiah658 says credit "is not needed but is
appreciated", so: thank you.

**Why Heavenly Loop is wired as the default, and the honest limit of that
choice.** It is the only one of the three whose author describes it as a
*seamless* loop built to hold up against repetition, which is the entire job
when there is one theme and it plays all evening. The other two are short loops
from a pack of four. **This was chosen on documented properties, not by
listening** — nobody on this side of the repo has heard any of them. The author
should listen to all three and swap or veto; that is what the candidates
directory is for.

To swap: copy the chosen candidate over `assets/audio/music/dusk_theme.ogg`.
Nothing in the code knows anything about the track except where it lives.

## Ambience and SFX — made here, not sourced

The three beds and the ten one-shots are **synthesised by
`tools/gen_placeholder_audio.py`**, not taken from a pack. They are ours, so
they are CC0 by construction and owe nobody attribution.

This is a deliberate departure from PLAN M10's "sources: CC0 packs (freesound,
Sonniss GDC)". Freesound needs an authenticated API and the Sonniss packs are
multi-gigabyte archives; neither is reachable from here. Rather than leave
thirteen slots empty, they are filled the same way the sprites, the dither
texture and the item icons already are — programmatically, as placeholders with
stable paths, so the real ones drop straight in. `ASSET_MANIFEST.md` lists every
one.

The generator is seeded, so re-running it produces byte-identical files and does
not show up as a diff.

| Group | Files | Path |
|---|---|---|
| Ambience beds (looping) | `sea`, `wind`, `hearth` | `assets/audio/ambience/` |
| One-shots | `gather`, `craft`, `place`, `milestone`, `bottle_open`, `page_turn`, `radial_tick`, `tandem_ready`, `beam`, `caught` | `assets/audio/sfx/` |
